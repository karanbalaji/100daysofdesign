## **About #100DaysOfDesign**  
🚀 The **#100DaysOfDesign** challenge is a **community-driven movement** designed to inspire, learn, and grow as designers and UX professionals. By fostering collaboration and sharing insights, we aim to evolve design as rapidly as the tech community does.  

### **Why I Started This Challenge**  
I wanted to:  
- Bring the design community together in a decentralized way.  
- Keep track of UX trends, tools, and mentor tips.  
- Inspire others to share, learn, and grow collectively.  

Join us as we explore, create, and advocate for design excellence!  

💬 **Discord Community**: [Join the conversation](https://discord.gg/sTsRXs3YuH)

---

## **The Challenge**  

1. **Learn & Document:** Write daily about a design topic or work on a project.  
2. **Connect & Learn:** Book mentors to gain new perspectives and network. Save at least one key tip from each mentor!  
3. **Build Resources:** Create a comprehensive documentation of design trends, tools, and insights on platforms like Medium, Substack, or Hashnode.  
4. **Support Others:** Share and promote other designers' work on platforms like LinkedIn, Twitter, or Threads.  
5. **Feedback Loop:** Engage in the Discord community to give and receive feedback anonymously, fostering constant improvement.
